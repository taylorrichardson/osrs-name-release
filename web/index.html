How the score works

Jagex frees a name if the account has been inactive for 2+ years and was permanently banned, never a member, or had bond-only membership that ended 5+ years ago. None of that is public, so this tool combines public signals:

  • Active, won't release: Sailing XP (skill launched Nov 2025), RuneMetrics activity in the last 2 years, or Wise Old Man shows XP gains in the last 2 years.
  • Ghost with no RuneMetrics profile, likely (85): not on either hiscore and no RS3 profile. In batch A, about 25% of these were claimed within a day.
  • Ghost, not yet checked on RuneMetrics, possible (62): about half turn out to have no profile.
  • Ghost with a RuneMetrics profile, unlikely: only about 1% were claimed in batch A.
  • Ranked free-to-play, unlikely: none of the 129 in batch A were released.
  • Ex-member, unlikely: about 0.3% were claimed in batch A.
  • Banned (Wise Old Man), unlikely: none of the 16 in batch A were claimed.

Value rates how desirable a name is (quality 0–100), separately from whether it will release. 1–2 character names score highest, and every 3-letter name rates at least high. Words score higher when they're short, well known and evocative (hand-picked cool words, OSRS terms). They score lower for filler words (the, which), web or tech jargon and abbreviations (html, login), hard-to-pronounce strings, and dull word forms (-tion, -ment, -ing, -ed, plurals). Tiers: extreme 78+, high 62+, medium 45+, low below 45.

These are estimates from public data. Jagex hasn't said which names are in each batch.
